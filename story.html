Our story

Lyngby Chinese School

Lyngby Chinese School is a community-based Chinese school in the northern part of the Greater Copenhagen area, founded by a group of passionate parents who wanted more children to have the opportunity to learn Chinese and connect with Chinese culture in a warm, encouraging setting.

Mission

Our purpose is to help children build confidence in Chinese language and cultural identity while learning in a way that feels engaging, practical and meaningful. The school supports students from beginning learners to those already with experience, helping them grow in reading, speaking, writing and cultural understanding.

What we teach

  • Chinese vocabulary, sentence structure and reading
  • Poetry, stories, seasonal traditions and cultural themes
  • Chinese character recognition and communication
  • Chinese heritage, festivals, rhythm and community

Our programmes

  • 0th grade to 9th grade classes
  • Chinese early years programme
  • AI Creative Applications class
  • Supportive weekend learning environment

Where and when

Classes take place every Saturday from 10:00 to 12:45 at Fuglsanggårdsskolen, Askevænget 10, 2830 Virum, Denmark.

The school follows a Saturday schedule with three teaching sessions and a short break in between.

Why it matters

At Lyngby Chinese School, students are invited to build a deeper connection to Chinese language and Chinese civilisation through structured teaching and cultural activities. We hope every learner can develop both practical language ability and a lasting sense of cultural belonging.
